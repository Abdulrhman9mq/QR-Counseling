Apixaban 5 mg

Anticoagulant

A medicine that helps prevent or treat blood clots.

Your prescribed dose depends on why you take apixaban and your individual clinical factors. Follow the exact instructions on your prescription label. Do not change your dose yourself.

Quick guide

💊

How to take

Swallow the tablet with water. Take it exactly as prescribed, usually twice daily when that is your prescribed regimen.

🕒

When to take

If prescribed twice daily, take doses in the morning and evening, about 12 hours apart. Try to take it at the same times each day.

🍽️

Food instructions

Apixaban can be taken with or without food.

⏰

Missed dose

Take the missed dose as soon as you remember on the same day, then continue your twice-daily schedule. Do not take extra doses to make up for a missed dose. If unsure, contact your pharmacist.

Bleeding: important warning

Apixaban can cause serious bleeding. Get urgent medical help for bleeding that will not stop, vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds, red or dark-brown urine, black tarry stools, coughing up blood, severe headache, fainting, or a significant fall or head injury.

Other side effects

Easy bruising and minor bleeding (such as nose or gum bleeding) may occur. Tell your healthcare professional about unusual or persistent bleeding, or any side effect that concerns you.

Precautions and interactions

  • Tell your doctor, dentist, and pharmacist that you take apixaban before procedures or dental work.
  • Do not stop apixaban without medical advice; stopping early may increase the risk of a blood clot.
  • Check with a pharmacist or doctor before starting any new medicine, including painkillers, aspirin, anti-inflammatory medicines, vitamins, or herbal products.
  • Avoid taking ibuprofen, naproxen, or aspirin unless your healthcare professional specifically advises you to.

Storage

Store at room temperature as directed on the product packaging. Keep out of reach of children.

Before you leave

Make sure you know your prescribed dose, what to do if you miss a dose, and which bleeding symptoms need urgent attention. Ask your pharmacist if anything is unclear.
